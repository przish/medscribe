## 1. Challenge & Local AI Definition
- **Theme**: **Local AI** — Useful AI experiences where meaningful AI computation happens on the user's device, rather than depending entirely on cloud inference (*Page 5*).
- **Core Challenge**: *"Build an AI product that remains genuinely useful when the cloud disappears"* (*Page 7*).
- **Problem Statement**: Solve a real problem; show why running AI locally creates an experience that would be difficult, expensive, slow, private, or impossible with a cloud-only approach (*Page 8*).
- **In-Scope Technologies**: Local LLMs, local vision models, local speech recognition, local embeddings/RAG, local AI agents, local image generation, edge AI, offline AI, privacy-preserving AI, hybrid local + cloud systems, AI on PCs/laptops/phones/edge hardware (*Page 6, 10*).
- **Tooling Flexibility**: No specific model, framework, OS, or hardware platform is required (*Page 10, 22*).

---

## 2. Technical & Integrity Rules
- **Meaningful Local Inference (Hard Rule)**: A meaningful part of AI inference must execute locally. The core product **must not depend entirely on a cloud AI API** (*Pages 6, 9, 22*).
- **Substantially Built During Event**: The project must be substantially built during the hackathon (starting Oct 9, 2:30 PM) (*Pages 3, 9, 22*).
- **Working Product**: A working product must be demonstrated (*Page 9*).
- **Live Deployment Not Required**: The app does not need to be hosted live, provided the repository has clear instructions for judges to recreate it locally (*Page 28*).
- **Disputes & Disqualification**: Pre-existing projects, receiving external help outside the hackathon participant list, or publishing fake benchmarks are explicit grounds for disqualification (*Pages 12, 13, 22, 27*).
- **Intellectual Property**: Teams retain 100% ownership of what they build (*Page 27*).

---

## 3. Judging Criteria (Weighting Breakdown)
*Half the score (50%) is usefulness and how real your Local AI is (Page 14).*

1. **Problem & Usefulness (25%)**: Does it solve a genuine problem for a clear target user?
2. **Local AI Implementation (25%)**: Is local inference fundamental, and does it give a meaningful advantage?
3. **Technical Execution (20%)**: Does it actually work, reliably enough for a live demonstration?
4. **Innovation (15%)**: Is it meaningfully different? Does Local AI enable something new?
5. **Product & Demo Quality (15%)**: Is the UX usable, and is the live demonstration convincing?
